# Market Gap Analysis: Sugar Traps vs. Nutritious Opportunities 📊✨

An interactive data analytics and visualization dashboard built with **Python** and **Streamlit** that maps over 52,000 food products to identify market mismatches, competitive spaces, and formulation opportunities.

🔗 **Live Application Link:** https://market-gap-analysis-dashboard-nimcurznlb8pstkkx4msfn.streamlit.app/

---

## 🚀 Project Overview & Business Value
In the modern consumer packaged goods (CPG) sector, products frequently leverage health-conscious branding while hiding poor nutritional profiles. This project processes data from the Open Food Facts database to systematically classify products into distinct market segments:
* **Sugar Traps 🚨:** Products high in marketing/appeal but carrying excessive sugar loads with minimal nutritional balance.
* **Market Goldmines ✨:** Highly nutritious, high-protein opportunities where market demand is growing but high-quality supply is limited.

This dashboard enables product strategy teams, competitive intelligence analysts, and food scientists to immediately spot uncompetitive market vacuums and guide corporate product positioning strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Engineering Pipeline
* **Language:** Python 3.13
* **Interface & Web Server:** Streamlit
* **Data Engineering & Manipulation:** Pandas
* **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn

### Core Engineering Milestones:
1. **Data Sanitization & Outlier Suppression:** Cleaned structural anomalies, eliminated extreme out-of-bounds metrics, and engineered standardized normalization scales.
2. **Feature Engineering:** Developed a multi-variable classification matrix parsing individual category sugar-to-protein density ratios.
3. **Interactive Implementation:** Transitioned static notebook modeling into a dynamic dashboard utilizing sidebar-driven component state filtering.
